Hiking Safety Tips for Hawaiʻi Island

Important Reminders

  • Always tell someone where you are going and when you expect to return.
  • Check the weather forecast before you hike — conditions can change quickly.
  • Bring plenty of water (at least 2 liters per person for moderate hikes).
  • Wear sturdy shoes with good traction.
  • Stay on marked trails — many areas have hidden lava tubes and cliffs.
  • Respect wildlife and do not feed animals.
  • Be aware of flash floods in valleys and near streams.

1. Site Name

Site Name: Hawaii Island Hiking Trails Guide

Tagline: Discover, Explore, and Hike Safely on Hawaiʻi Island

Why I chose this name: It is clear, descriptive, and tells visitors exactly what the website offers. It focuses on Hawaiʻi Island (the Big Island) and emphasizes guidance and safety.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to serve as a helpful informational resource for locals and visitors who want to explore the best hiking trails on Hawaiʻi Island.

It will provide trail details, an interactive trail filter, safety information, and a dynamic "Plan Your Hike" tool to help users make informed and safe hiking decisions.

3. Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: A visitor new to the Big Island wants to know which easy trails are suitable for beginners and families. They use the filter to see only "Easy" trails and check estimated time and required gear.
  • Scenario 2: A local hiker is planning a weekend adventure and needs moderate to hard trails near Volcanoes National Park. They want photos, distance, elevation, and safety tips before heading out.
  • Scenario 3: Someone wants to know what to pack for a full-day hike and uses the "Plan Your Hike" form to get a personalized packing checklist.

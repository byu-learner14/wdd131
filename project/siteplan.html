Hawaii Island Hiking Trails Guide

🌋 Discover • Explore • Hike Safely on Hawaiʻi Island 🌺

1. Site Name

Site Name: Hawaii Island Hiking Trails Guide

Tagline: Discover, Explore, and Hike Safely on Hawaiʻi Island

Why I chose this name: It is clear, descriptive, and immediately tells visitors what the website offers. It focuses specifically on Hawaiʻi Island (the Big Island) while emphasizing safe and informed exploration.

Optional domain: hawaiihikingtrails.com (for future use)

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide a helpful, informative resource for both locals and visitors interested in hiking the best trails on Hawaiʻi Island.

It features detailed trail information, an interactive filter by difficulty and region, safety tips, and a dynamic "Plan Your Hike" tool that generates personalized recommendations using JavaScript.

The site consists of multiple pages (Home, Trails, Safety Tips, Plan Your Hike) with consistent navigation and a natural Hawaiian-inspired design.

3. Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: A first-time visitor to the Big Island wants easy trails suitable for beginners or families. They use the interactive filter to view only "Easy" trails and check estimated time and gear needs.
  • Scenario 2: An experienced local hiker plans a weekend trip and needs moderate or hard trails near Hawaiʻi Volcanoes National Park, including photos, distance, elevation gain, and important safety information.
  • Scenario 3: A hiker preparing for a full-day adventure uses the "Plan Your Hike" form to receive a customized packing checklist and estimated hiking time based on their selections.

5. Typography

  • Headings: Georgia, serif — warm, natural, and trustworthy for titles and section headings.
  • Body Text: Segoe UI, system-ui, sans-serif — clean, modern, and highly readable for paragraphs and lists.

This combination creates an inviting, adventurous feel while maintaining excellent readability across all devices.

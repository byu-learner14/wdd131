About Hawaii

Hawaii is a volcanic archipelago in the central Pacific Ocean. Known for its tropical climate, beautiful beaches, lush rainforests, and rich Polynesian culture, it’s a dream destination for millions every year.

  • 🌋 Active volcanoes on the Big Island

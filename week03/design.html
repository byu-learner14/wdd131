Design Principles

Applying Design Principles

Good design is obvious. Great design is transparent. Effective web design follows proven principles that guide the user's eye and make content easy to understand and interact with.

Key principles include contrast, repetition, alignment, proximity, hierarchy, and balance. When applied well, they create intuitive, beautiful, and accessible experiences.

Important Note

Always check color contrast! Poor contrast makes text hard to read, especially for users with visual impairments.
